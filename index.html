Seeing "Windows protected your PC"?

That screen appears because the launcher is not digitally signed yet. A code-signing certificate costs hundreds of dollars a year, and Windows shows this warning for every new program without one, whatever is inside it.

Click More info, then Run anyway. You only need to do this once.

Questions

Do I need to own Minecraft?

Yes. The launcher signs in with your Microsoft account and requires Minecraft: Java Edition.

Is it free?

Yes, the launcher is completely free.

Why is the first launch slow?

The first time you play a version, the launcher downloads Minecraft, Java and the mod loader. After that it starts quickly.

Will my skin show on other servers?

Yes. The skin is saved to your Minecraft account, so it shows on every online-mode server and in every launcher.

Mac or Linux?

Right now the download is for Windows. Other systems may come later.

I found a bug. Where do I report it?

Open a ticket in our Discord with a screenshot of the error.
